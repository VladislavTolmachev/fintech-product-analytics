Fintech Product Analytics

Credit card activation, first-purchase conversion and retention — a hands-on SQL case built with reproducible synthetic data. Designed to demonstrate the workflow of a junior product analyst, from business questions to metric definitions and data-quality checks.

Questions this project investigates

  1. Where does conversion drop across signup, application, approval, issuance, activation and first purchase?
  2. How long does it take an activated card to make its first successful purchase?
  3. How does monthly retention evolve across signup cohorts?
  4. Which acquisition channels show different downstream conversion patterns?
  5. Which metrics would help a product team prioritize improvements?

Validation status

Synthetic CSV generation and referential/time-order checks passed locally. SQL scripts have not yet been executed in PostgreSQL, so final findings and product recommendations are deliberately pending. No unverified business conclusions are presented.

Session timing and transaction timing are simplified by the generator; retention and purchase-latency patterns are illustrative artifacts, not realistic product estimates.

Important: all data is synthetic and this independent exercise does not use or represent Plata's internal data.
